QUESTIONS FROM THE CRYPT

Asked at the gate.

What counts as a dead token?

An Elysium token admitted to the graveyard list: trading has stopped or collapsed, and its pool sits idle. Admission is curated by the project and announced here and on X first.

When does Revive open?

When the Revive Reserve holds real Z0MBIT bought with creator fees that have actually arrived. Watch the reserve counter on this page. No date is promised against money that has not landed.

What are the fees, exactly?

Trading fee is 3% in total: 2.6% creator, 0.4% protocol, 0% LP while the pool is below the LP line. Past the line: 2.1% creator, 0.4% protocol, 0.5% LP. Revive itself charges no fee; the 20% bonus is paid by the reserve, not by you.

Where does Z0MBIT trade?

On Montra, in a Uniswap v4 pool on Elysium, with the full supply of 1,000,000,000 locked in the pool at launch. Token contract is announced on this page and on X at launch. Trust only those two sources.
